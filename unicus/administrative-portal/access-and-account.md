# Access and your account

## Sign in

Sign in to the [administrative portal](https://app.idunicus.com/) with your
e-mail and password. If your company has its own address (for example
`yourcompany.idunicus.com`), you can sign in there and see your company's logo
and colour. Tekbees assigns the address; administrators find it in
**Company → Appearance → Your portal**.

Several failed attempts in a row lock the account for a few minutes. If your
account or your company is deactivated, the portal says so: talk to an
administrator of your company.

### E-mailed code

After the password, the portal may ask for a **6-digit code** sent to your
e-mail. It is asked of administrators and, if your company turned it on in
**Security**, of every user.

* The code expires within minutes; you can request another.
* Tick **Remember this device** so you do not have to type it again in that
  browser for a few days.

### Access only from your network

If your company restricted access to its IP addresses, you can only sign in
from them (see [Company settings](company-settings.md#security)).

## Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen select **Forgot your password?**, type your e-mail and
open the link you receive. The link expires in 30 minutes and works once.

Passwords need at least 8 characters, with upper-case and lower-case letters,
numbers and a symbol.

{% hint style="info" %}
An administrator can also send you the password reset e-mail from **Users**.
Nobody can see your password or type it for you.
{% endhint %}

## Your profile

Open the menu with your name (bottom left) → **My profile and photo**:

* **Personal data:** your name. Only an administrator can change your e-mail.
* **Profile photo:** JPG, PNG or WebP up to 2 MB.
* **Change password:** saving it signs you out of every session.
* **Sessions and devices:** the browsers where you are signed in. Close one,
  or all but this one, if you do not recognise it.

The same menu changes the language (Spanish or English) and the theme (light
or dark), and signs you out.

## Help

The **?** button in the top bar opens this documentation and the Tekbees
service desk.
