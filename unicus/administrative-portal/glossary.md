# Glossary

* Country: Select a specific nation to know the procedures performed.
* Filter: specific search by user data or characteristics.
* Date: day, month and year in which the procedure is performed.
* Time: time when the procedure is performed.
* Actions: details (user information or documents) and option to delete it.
* Status: active or inactive.
* Actions: details (information), activate or deactivate function and delete role.
* First and last name: complete identification information per registered user.
