# Company registration

Registration starts with an **invitation from Tekbees**, sent to the e-mail of
the person who will administer the account. If you do not have one yet, write
to your sales contact or to [Support](../sdk-web-v5/support.md).

## 1. Open the invitation

The e-mail carries a personal link with an expiry date (the page shows it).
When you open it, confirm your e-mail: request the 6-digit code sent to that
same address and type it in. The code expires within minutes; if it does,
request a new one.

Your changes are saved automatically while you fill in the form: you can close
it and come back with the same link before it expires.

## 2. Fill in your details

| Step | What is asked |
| --- | --- |
| **Company** | Legal name, trade name, tax ID, country, city, address, phone, website and sector. |
| **Administrator** | Names, last names, e-mail, ID document, position and phone of the person who will administer the account. Optional: a technical contact. |
| **Brand** | Logo (optional; PNG, JPG, WEBP or SVG up to 2 MB) and the background, button and text colours, with a preview of what the person will see. |
| **Integration** | Your webhook URL (optional, `https://`), how many verifications you expect per month, the countries where you will operate and, for each country, the **Documents you will accept** (Tekbees enables them when it approves your registration), and comments. |
| **Review and submit** | Check everything, accept the processing of personal data and select **Submit for review**. |

No password is created in this form.

## 3. Review by Tekbees

Once you submit, the link closes and Tekbees reviews the information. You will
receive one of these answers by e-mail:

* **Approved.** Your company is created, and the administrator receives an
  e-mail to create their password and sign in to the portal. The company
  starts in a **trial period**; Tekbees moves it to production when
  appropriate.
* **Changes requested.** A new link arrives with what needs to be corrected;
  the old one stops working.
* **Not approved.** The link stays closed.
